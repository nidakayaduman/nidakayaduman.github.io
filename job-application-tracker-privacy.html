Privacy Policy for Job Application Tracker

Last Updated: October 17, 2025

Thank you for using Job Application Tracker (“the App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application available on iOS and Android.

1. Developer Information

Developer: Nida Kayaduman
Contact Email: elifnida@gmail.com

2. Overview of Data Practices

Job Application Tracker is an offline-first personal tool. All personal information you enter (such as company names, job titles, notes, or application details) is stored only on your own device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this information on our servers. We cannot access your personal application data in any way.

3. Information You Provide (Stored Locally on Your Device)

  • Company Names
  • Job Titles (including application positions and interview step titles)
  • Notes (free-form text entered by you)
  • Application Data (e.g., application dates, status, progress)

This data is never uploaded or shared with us or any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob (Advertising): We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS / GAID on Android)
  • Approximate location (from IP address)
  • Device type and model
  • Mobile OS version
  • IP address

This information is processed by Google, not by us. For more details, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. What the App Does NOT Do

  • Collect precise or real-time GPS location
  • Access contacts, camera, or photo gallery
  • Use social media logins
  • Process payments or sell products
  • Send emails or newsletters
  • Use analytics beyond AdMob SDK
  • Collect data from or market to children under 13

6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under GDPR, we rely on:

  • Legitimate Interest: to display advertising (via AdMob)
  • Consent: users can withdraw ad tracking consent in device settings

All personal information stays on your device; we do not process personal data as a controller under GDPR.

7. Your Rights under GDPR

EU/EEA residents can request access, correction, or deletion of data processed by AdMob via Google support.

8. Your Rights under CCPA / CPRA (California)

California residents have the right to know, delete, and opt-out of sale of data collected by AdMob. We do not sell or share data ourselves.

9. Compliance with CalOPPA

This Privacy Policy link is publicly accessible in our app store listings. Users will be notified of material changes, and the last updated date will reflect revisions.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

All user data is stored locally. Deleting the app or clearing app data permanently erases it.

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under 13.

12. Security

Data is stored locally; securing your device (lock screen, Face ID, etc.) helps protect your data.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy; any changes will appear here with an updated date.
