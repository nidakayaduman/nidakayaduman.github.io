Privacy Policy for Twin Shift: Mirror Puzzle

A clear overview of how this iOS puzzle game handles gameplay data, advertising, consent, and privacy choices.

Effective Date: July 2026

This date can be updated before publishing this page as the App Store Connect Privacy Policy URL.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Twin Shift: Mirror Puzzle ("the App"), published by Elif Nida Kayaduman ("we," "us," or "the developer"), handles information when you use the App.

Twin Shift: Mirror Puzzle is a mobile puzzle game with gameplay levels, hints, undo features, progression, landmark unlocks, rewarded ads, rewarded landmark unlock ads, and interstitial ads. This policy describes local gameplay data, advertising, consent choices, and related privacy practices.

2. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

The App does not require account creation and does not ask users to submit personal profile information through forms or accounts.

  • No account creation is required.
  • We do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or physical address through the App.
  • The App does not request user-generated photos or videos.
  • The App does not request access to your contacts.
  • The App does not directly request precise location from the user.
  • The developer does not collect payment information for this App.

This does not mean that no data is ever processed. Third-party advertising services used in the App may collect or process certain information as described below.

3. Information Stored Locally on the Device

The App may store gameplay and preference data locally on your device using Apple/iOS local storage such as UserDefaults or similar local persistence. This local data is mainly used to make the game work correctly and remember your progress.

  • Completed levels and gameplay progression
  • Unlocked landmarks and early unlocked landmarks
  • Hint credits and ad reward state
  • Undo status, including free undo status where applicable
  • Game settings and rating prompt state
  • Privacy or consent state where applicable

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display ads. The App may also use the Google User Messaging Platform SDK to request and manage consent where required.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain data for ad serving, fraud prevention, reporting, frequency capping, measurement, and related purposes. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google/AdMob configuration, this may include:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers where permitted
  • Approximate location inferred by Google or AdMob where applicable
  • Ad interactions and ad performance information
  • Diagnostics and crash-related information
  • IP address
  • App activity related to ad serving

Data handling by Google and its partners is governed by their own policies and settings. Some ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and advertising configuration.

The App does not use banner ads and does not use app open ads.

5. Rewarded Ads

You may choose to watch rewarded ads or rewarded interstitial ads to receive optional in-game benefits, such as hint credits or early landmark unlocks.

Rewarded ads are optional. If you choose not to watch a rewarded ad, you can continue playing normally, although the optional reward may not be granted.

6. Interstitial Ads

Interstitial ads may be shown at natural breaks in the game, such as after completing levels. They are intended to appear between gameplay moments and not during active gameplay.

Interstitial ads are separate from optional rewarded ads. They are not used to unlock landmarks.

7. Consent and Privacy Choices

In regions where consent is required, the App may show a consent form through Google User Messaging Platform. Your consent choices may affect whether advertising is personalized or non-personalized.

If implemented in the App, you may be able to manage privacy choices through the App settings. You can also manage ad tracking and personalization through your iOS device settings.

8. App Tracking Transparency and Device Settings

iOS may allow you to limit tracking through device settings. If the App requests tracking permission, you can allow or deny that permission.

Where required by Apple's policies, the App will request permission before accessing tracking-related identifiers. Denying permission does not prevent the game from being played. Ads may still appear, but they may be less personalized.

The App may use SKAdNetwork or other Apple advertising attribution technologies as required for advertising measurement.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through account registration, forms, or direct user submissions.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to the developer, please contact us so we can review and address the request.

10. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell user personal information.

Data may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google AdMob for advertising, consent management, fraud prevention, reporting, and analytics related to ads. We may also disclose information if required to comply with legal obligations, enforce terms, or protect rights and safety in limited circumstances.

11. Data Retention

Local gameplay progress remains on your device unless you delete the App, reset the relevant app data, or the App provides a feature to clear it.

Ad-related data is handled by Google and AdMob according to their own policies. The developer does not maintain a user account database for this App.

12. Security

We make reasonable efforts to protect the App and minimize direct personal data collection. However, no method of electronic storage, processing, or transmission is 100% secure.

13. International Availability

The App may be available globally. Third-party services such as Google AdMob may process data in different countries, depending on their infrastructure, policies, and legal requirements.

Users should review Google's privacy terms for more information about ad-related processing.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, the Effective Date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of the App after changes are posted means the updated policy applies.
