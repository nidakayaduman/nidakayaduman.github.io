Privacy Policy for RetroN VintageCam

Last Updated: December 2025

1. Developer Information

Developer: Elif Nida Kayaduman
Contact Email: elifnida@gmail.com

2. Overview

RetroN VintageCam is a retro-style camera and photo editing application. Most features work offline, and image processing is performed locally on your device. The developer does not operate independent servers and does not store personal data.

Limited network requests may occur only for essential technical or advertising-related purposes, as described below.

3. Information Processed Locally

The app processes the following data locally on your device:

  • Photos you capture or select
  • Filters, frames, and effects you apply
  • Optional custom date stamps
  • Optional city or area label derived from location (if permission is granted)
  • Locally stored Memories metadata

This information is not uploaded to developer-controlled servers.

4. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Photos: To select and save edited images.
  • Camera: To capture photos within the app.
  • Location (Optional): Used only to display a city or place name on saved photos.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): To request consent for personalized advertising.

5. Network Requests & Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA), if user consent is given
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device type and operating system information
  • IP address for fraud prevention and ad delivery

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. The developer does not receive or store this data.

Connectivity Check

The app may perform a lightweight network request to a Google connectivity endpoint to verify internet access and support basic app functionality.

  • No user-identifiable information is intentionally transmitted
  • The request may expose the device’s IP address to Google
  • No response data is stored or processed by the app

6. Location Data & Reverse Geocoding

If you grant location permission, the app may access your device’s location at the time a photo is saved in order to determine a city or area name.

  • Location access is optional and fully user-controlled
  • Used only for labeling photos with a city or place name
  • May involve Apple or system-level reverse geocoding services
  • Location data is not stored by the developer

7. What the App Does NOT Do

  • Does not upload photos to developer servers
  • Does not sell personal data
  • Does not track precise or continuous location
  • Does not collect personally identifiable information
  • Does not access social media accounts

8. GDPR (EU Users)

Advertising consent is managed via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework and Google’s User Messaging Platform where applicable.

9. CCPA / CPRA

RetroN VintageCam does not sell or share personal information. California users may manage advertising preferences through Google’s privacy controls.

10. CalOPPA

This Privacy Policy is publicly accessible and will be updated if practices change.

11. Data Retention

All data remains on your device. Deleting the app permanently removes stored data.

12. Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

13. Security

Data is protected using iOS system security features such as sandboxing, device passcodes, and biometric authentication.

14. Policy Changes

Any updates to this policy will be reflected by revising the “Last Updated” date.
