Privacy Policy for Unfollow Radar

Last Updated: October 26, 2025

Thank you for using Unfollow Radar (“the App”). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our iOS application.

1. Developer Information

Developer: Nida Kayaduman
Contact Email: elifnida@gmail.com

2. Overview of Data Practices

Unfollow Radar is primarily an offline-first analysis tool. User-imported data is processed locally on the device and is not uploaded to any server owned by the developer. Limited network requests may occur only for specific technical or advertising-related purposes, as described below.

3. Information Processed Locally

  • Follower usernames
  • Following usernames
  • Difference analysis (mutual, fans, not-following-back)

This data remains on your device at all times and is never transmitted to the developer.

4. Network Requests and Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA), subject to user consent (ATT)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Device type, OS version
  • IP address and ad interaction data

This data is collected and processed by Google. The developer does not access or store this information. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Google Connectivity Check (Time Synchronization)

The app makes a lightweight network request to https://www.google.com/generate_204 to verify internet connectivity and ensure accurate daily usage limit resets.

  • No personal identifiers are intentionally transmitted
  • The request may expose the device IP address to Google
  • The response contains no content and is not stored

5. Location Data and Reverse Geocoding

When saving photos, the app may request access to your device’s location only if permission is granted. Location data is used solely to determine the city name via reverse geocoding.

  • Location access is optional and user-controlled
  • Used only for contextual labeling (e.g., city name)
  • May involve Apple or system-level geocoding services
  • No location data is stored by the developer

6. What the App Does NOT Do

  • Does NOT access your Instagram account or API
  • Does NOT upload or share imported files
  • Does NOT store personal data on developer servers
  • Does NOT track precise location continuously
  • Does NOT perform background tracking

7. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Consent: Advertising tracking via ATT
  • Legitimate Interest: Limited connectivity checks and app functionality

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally processed data is deleted when the app is removed. The developer does not retain user data.

9. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended for children under the age of 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be reflected by revising the “Last Updated” date above.
