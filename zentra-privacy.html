01

Information We Collect

Script Content

All scripts and text you enter into Zentra Prompter are stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, access, transmit, or store your script content on any external servers.

Device & Usage Information

We may collect limited, non-personal technical data automatically. This data does not identify you directly.

Device type and model
Operating system version
App version
Crash logs and diagnostic information
Basic usage analytics (non-identifiable)

Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version of Zentra Prompter may display ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process the following data:

Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
IP address
Ad interaction data
Approximate location derived from IP address

This data may be used to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your device settings and consent preferences. You can manage personalized advertising under Settings → Privacy → Tracking on your device.

Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases — Zentra Pro

Optional Zentra Pro purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Data Storage

Your scripts and personal content remain stored locally on your device. If you use Apple's iCloud backup, your data may be backed up through Apple's services. Zentra Prompter does not operate any independent cloud storage servers.

04

Zentra Prompter may integrate with the following third-party services, each operating under their own privacy policies:

Apple App Store — for purchases and subscriptions
Google AdMob — for advertising
Apple Analytics — if enabled by the user

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect the integrity of the app. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

06

Children's Privacy

Zentra Prompter is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR or CCPA), you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

The right to request deletion

Since Zentra Prompter does not directly collect personal data, most requests may not apply. For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact us using the details below.
