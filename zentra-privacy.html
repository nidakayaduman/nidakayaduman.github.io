Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Zentra Prompter is developed and operated by Nida Kayaduman.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use Zentra Prompter.

1. Information We Collect

a. Script Content

All scripts and text entered into Zentra Prompter are stored locally on your device. We do not collect, access, transmit, or store your script content on external servers.

b. Device & Usage Information

We may collect limited non-personal technical information automatically, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information
  • Basic usage analytics (non-identifiable)

This data does not directly identify you.

c. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Zentra Prompter may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain data, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data
  • Approximate location derived from IP address

This data may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your device settings and consent preferences.

Google's Privacy Policy can be found here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can control personalized advertising in your device settings under Privacy > Tracking.

d. In-App Purchases (Zentra Pro)

Zentra Prompter offers optional in-app purchases for Zentra Pro features. All payments are processed securely through Apple’s App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Improve app stability and performance
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Enhance user experience
  • Provide advertising in the free version

3. Data Storage

Your scripts and personal content remain stored locally on your device unless you choose to back up your device using Apple services (such as iCloud). Zentra Prompter does not operate independent cloud storage servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Zentra Prompter may use the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store (for purchases and subscriptions)
  • Google AdMob (for advertising)
  • Apple analytics services (if enabled by the user)

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect the integrity of the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Zentra Prompter is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights regarding your personal data, such as:

  • The right to access your data
  • The right to request deletion
  • The right to restrict processing
  • The right to withdraw consent for advertising tracking

Since Zentra Prompter does not directly collect personal data, most requests may not apply. However, you may contact us for any privacy-related inquiries.
