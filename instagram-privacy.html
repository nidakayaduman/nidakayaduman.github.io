02

Overview of Data Practices

Unfollow Radar is an offline-first analysis tool. It lets you compare your Instagram followers and following lists by importing JSON or HTML files exported from your Instagram account. All data is processed locally on your device — we do not collect, transmit, or store any of this data on our servers.
03

Information Processed Locally

The following data is processed only within the app on your device. No external server or cloud storage is involved.

Follower usernames
Following usernames
Difference analysis (mutual, fans, not-following-back)
04

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob — Advertising

Used to display banner and interstitial ads. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising Identifier (IDFA)
Approximate location derived from IP address
Device type and OS version
IP address and usage data for ad delivery
05

What the App Does NOT Do

Does NOT access your Instagram account directly
Does NOT connect to Instagram's API
Does NOT upload or share your files
Does NOT access contacts, photos, or camera
Does NOT track precise location
Does NOT collect any analytics beyond AdMob
06

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Consent — requested via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework

Your Rights under GDPR
