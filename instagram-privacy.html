Privacy Policy for Unfollow Radar

Last Updated: October 26, 2025

Thank you for using Unfollow Radar (“the App”). This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application available on iOS.

1. Developer Information

Developer: Nida Kayaduman
Contact Email: elifnida@gmail.com

2. Overview of Data Practices

Unfollow Radar is an offline-first analysis tool. The app allows you to compare your Instagram followers and following lists by importing JSON or HTML files that you manually export from your Instagram account. All data is processed locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this data on our servers.

3. Information Processed Locally

  • Follower usernames
  • Following usernames
  • Difference analysis (mutual, fans, not-following-back)

All processing happens only within the app on your own device. No external server or cloud storage is involved.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party SDK:

Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA)
  • Approximate location (from IP address)
  • Device type and OS version
  • IP address and usage data for ad delivery

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. For more details, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. What the App Does NOT Do

  • Does NOT access your Instagram account directly
  • Does NOT connect to Instagram’s API
  • Does NOT upload or share your files
  • Does NOT access contacts, photos, or camera
  • Does NOT track precise location
  • Does NOT collect any analytics beyond AdMob

6. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Under GDPR, data processing is based on:

  • Legitimate Interest: to display advertising (via AdMob)
  • Consent: requested via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework

7. Your Rights under GDPR

Since no personal data is collected by the developer, any requests regarding advertising data should be directed to Google AdMob Support.

8. Your Rights under CCPA / CPRA

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information. California users may manage data used by AdMob through Google’s privacy controls.

9. Compliance with CalOPPA

This Privacy Policy is publicly accessible from our App Store listing. Users will be notified of updates through an updated "Last Updated" date.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

All data exists only locally on your device. Deleting the app or its data permanently erases all information.

11. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13 years old and does not knowingly collect data from them.

12. Security

Data is stored locally on your device. Using device-level security (Face ID, passcode) helps protect your information.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will appear here with an updated date at the top of this page.
